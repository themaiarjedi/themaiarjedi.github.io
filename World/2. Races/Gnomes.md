The Gnomes are 
##### Lifespan:
300 - 400 years. The average Gnome reaches maturity at age 24.
##### Height:
3' - 4' (Forest Gnomes are 2' - 3')
##### Weight:
40 - 50 lbs (Forest Gnomes are 30-40 lbs)
##### Vision:
Gnomes are able to see with minimal light. A single torch would allow them to see 100' in a deep cavern. They cannot see in the total darkness of a deep cave as Dwarves do, and they would need moonlight to see at night (unlike Elves who can see by the light of the stars).
