The Akwelin are bird-like humanoids. They most commonly have eagle or hawk-like features.
Their feathers range from shades of brown to white, the rarest being all-white.
Akwelin's wings are powerful enough for them to carry more than twice their bodyweight (they can carry someone while flying without much, if any, difficulty).
##### Lifespan:
70 - 80 years. The average Akwelin reaches maturity at age 13.
##### Height:
5' - 7'
##### Weight:
95 - 190 lbs
##### Vision:
Akwelin can identify small details from 30-ft (details a human would need to be within 5-ft to see), and can identify something as small as a rabbit from 3 miles away. They have difficulty seeing in the dark however, as their vision becomes blurry in low-light.
