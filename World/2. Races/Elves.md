The Elves are
##### Lifespan:
Elves live until they are killed, or until they choose to forfeit their immortality. At which point they begin aging and live another 40-60 years. The average Elf reaches maturity at age 75.
##### Height:
6' - 8'
##### Weight:
100 - 150 lbs
##### Vision:
Elves can see a starlit night as well as if it was mid-day, and can make out small details at roughly twice the distance a human could clearly see. They cannot see in the total darkness of a deep cave as Dwarves do.
