The Dwarves are 
##### Lifespan:
250 - 300 years. The average Dwarf reaches maturity at age 45.
##### Height:
4' - 5'
##### Weight:
150 - 220 lbs
##### Vision:
Dark-Vision. Dwarves in a deep cave (with no light source) can see as well as though the space was well-lit. Their vision blurs in bright light (a bright day, a brightly lit gala) unless they wear eye protection.
