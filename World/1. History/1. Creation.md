[[0. Vhaldir]] was alone in the Void for eons, and decided to create the universe as a sandbox to play with Creation. After creating countless galaxies on his own,  [[0. Vhaldir]] created the seven gods to aid him. These new gods immediately began forming a new galaxy, and it was unlike any of [[0. Vhaldir]]'s creations. The gods worked together, and after creating billions of planetary systems [[0. Vhaldir]] gave them sentience. This once again caused the gods to begin working in yet another way, now playing with each other and their play heightened their desire for further creativity. It was then they created a new star, orbited by a lone planet... [[Wyrdrin]].
